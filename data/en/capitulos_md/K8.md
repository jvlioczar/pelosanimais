# K8. "Radical veganism pushes people away"

**Thesis:** there may be some strategic truth here: certain tones and tactics put some people off. But that isn't a moral refutation, nor a personal excuse. That a messenger is unpleasant doesn't make the message false. And research on social movements shows that radical voices can even increase support for moderate ones, the so-called radical flank effect.

**Family:** K. Effectiveness and individual responsibility · **Part:** IND

## 1. Formulation

**Everyday version:** "You scare people off. Why don't you just promote reduction? Toxic veganism."

**Strongest version (steelman):**

1. **P1.** Messages perceived as radical or judgmental provoke rejection.
2. **P2.** Rejection reduces people's willingness to change.
3. **P3.** A more moderate approach, such as encouraging reduction, would reach more people.
4. **C.** So radical veganism harms its own cause.

P1 and P2 have support in some contexts. P3 is a plausible strategic hypothesis. The problem is that the conclusion, even if correct, is about strategy, not about whether it's right to eat animals.

The argument is **valid** as a strategic criticism. It fails when used as a justification for not changing.

## 3. Answer for children

Sometimes a person speaks in a way that makes us angry, even when they're right. But the way they speak doesn't change whether something is right or wrong. If someone rudely warns us that we're hurting someone, it's still worth stopping.

## 4. Short answer

It may be that some tones and tactics put some people off, and it's worth discussing the best way to communicate. But that doesn't change whether it's right to eat animals. An unpleasant messenger doesn't make the message false. And research on social movements suggests radical voices can even increase support for moderate ones. The question for you remains: do animals deserve to be spared, regardless of who speaks for them?

**Question to ask back:** "If a vegan were kind to you, would the answer about the animals change?"

## 5. Full answer

### (a) Reconstruction and diagnosis

The argument mixes a strategic question with a moral one:

| Question | Type | Does the argument answer it? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What's the best way to persuade people? | Strategic | Partly |
| Is it right to eat animals? | Moral | No |

### (b) Where the argument breaks

**1. The form doesn't change the content.** That a message is delivered unpleasantly doesn't make it false.

**2. It isn't a personal excuse.** Other people's tone doesn't excuse anyone from examining the question for themselves.

**3. The radical flank effect can be positive.** In 2022, experiments with more than 2,700 participants, including one about the animal movement, found that the presence of a radical group increased support for moderate groups in the same movement, who came to seem more reasonable by comparison.

**4. "Radical" often just means consistent.** Saying animals shouldn't be killed without need is called radical, but it applies a principle almost everyone accepts.

**5. Eating more meat out of irritation is a curious effect.** If irritation with vegans leads someone to harm animals more, the problem isn't with the animals.

### (c) Data and sources

| Source | What it says | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Simpson, Willer and Feinberg, 2022, *PNAS Nexus*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9896934/) | The presence of a radical flank increased support for moderate groups in the same movement, including the animal movement | Perception experiments; the effect comes from tactics, not goals |

### (d) The strongest counter-reply: strategy matters for the animals

The most serious version says that if the goal is to help animals, the effectiveness of communication is a moral question, because it determines how many animals are spared.

### (e) Why the counter-reply doesn't reach the conclusion

1. **It's valid for activists.** Animal advocates should think about the best strategy.
2. **It doesn't apply to whoever receives the message.** For that person, the question is still about their own plate.
3. **Different strategies can coexist.** Moderate and firmer voices can complement each other.

### (f) Back to the moral question

The argument is about vegans, not animals. But animals don't choose who speaks for them. The moral question stays the same, whoever the messenger: is it fair to make them suffer and die when we don't need to?

| Level | Error | How it appears in K8 |

| Logical | Attacking the messenger | Assesses who speaks, not what's said |
| Logical | Changing the subject | From the moral question to the strategic one |

## 8. Contradictions

1. **Accepts ideas defended by unpleasant people on other subjects.**
2. **Doesn't change opinion on other matters because of tone.**
3. **Says they'd support a moderate approach.** But doesn't change when they meet one.

## 9. Biases

| Bias | How it sustains K8 | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Psychological reactance | Feeling pressured provokes rejection | General literature on reactance |
| Stereotype | All vegans are seen as radicals | Linked to L1 |
| Reactive devaluation | The same idea sounds reasonable from a moderate and radical from an activist | [Ross and Stillinger, 1991](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1571-9979.1991.tb00634.x) |

## 10. Extrapolations

The principle being tested is: "if a message is delivered radically, I needn't consider it".

| Applied to | What would follow | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Radical abolitionists | Slavery wouldn't need questioning | Absurd |
| Climate activists | Climate change wouldn't need to be taken seriously | No sensible person concludes this |
| Vegans | The question of animals wouldn't need considering | The same error |

## 11. What the argument gets right

The way we communicate matters, and some animal advocates do push people away with judgment, aggression or contempt. Discussing communication strategies is legitimate and necessary, and the movement should learn from criticism.

But criticism of the messenger doesn't answer the message. Whatever the tone of whoever speaks, the question about the animals is still waiting for an answer.
